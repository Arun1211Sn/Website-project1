About Thirupathi S.T.S Groups Realty

A Chennai-based partner for land, property, and site-maintenance services.

Who we are

Thirupathi S.T.S Groups Realty is a Chennai-based firm offering end-to-end services for buyers, sellers, and owners of land. Founded and led by S. Thirupathi Rajan, we serve clients across Chennai and the surrounding districts of Tamil Nadu. Our office is located in Thoraipakkam, off the OMR corridor — one of the most active real-estate zones in South Chennai.

The firm operates as a single point of contact for people who would otherwise have to coordinate with several independent vendors: an agent to find land, an advocate to verify documents, a surveyor to measure, a contractor to clean the site, and a painter to finish a building. We consolidate these into one relationship so clients can focus on decisions instead of logistics.

Services we provide

Our work falls into three broad buckets:

Land and property

  • Buying and selling residential land
  • Approved plot transactions (DTCP / CMDA layouts)
  • Farm land listings across Tamil Nadu
  • Documentation support — title search, encumbrance certificate, patta transfer, registration

Site preparation

  • Vacant land cleaning and debris removal
  • Land levelling, grading, and sand filling
  • Tree cutting and root removal on residential plots and farm land

Building maintenance

  • Interior and exterior painting for homes, apartments, and commercial buildings
  • Waterproof coatings and weather-resistant finishes

How we work

Most of our clients come through word of mouth from previous customers. Every engagement starts with a phone call or a site visit — we like to understand the land, the owner's intent, and the surrounding context before quoting anything. For land transactions, we explain the documentation trail in plain language so the buyer knows what they are signing at each step.

We believe that clarity saves money. Many first-time buyers in Chennai lose time and capital because they rush past document verification, miss a DTCP approval line item, or underestimate site-preparation costs on a plot that looks level from the road. Our role is to slow that process down at the right moments and speed it up at the rest.

Service areas

We primarily operate in and around:

  • South Chennai — Thoraipakkam, Sholinganallur, OMR (IT Corridor), Siruseri, Kelambakkam, Thaiyur
  • West and Southwest Chennai — Tambaram, Chromepet, Pallavaram, Selaiyur
  • Farm land belt — Kancheepuram, Chengalpattu, Tiruvallur, Vellore, Villupuram

For projects outside these areas we take on a case-by-case basis, depending on the scope and the travel involved.

Why clients choose us

  • One point of contact. You deal with a single team from enquiry to registration.
  • Document-first approach. We verify patta, chitta, EC, and layout approvals before price discussions, not after.
  • Transparent pricing. No hidden brokerage structures — you know what goes to the seller and what goes to us.
  • Local knowledge. Over a decade of on-the-ground work in Chennai's fast-changing peri-urban zones.
  • After-sale services. Cleaning, tree cutting, and painting can all be coordinated after you take possession, so the plot or house is truly move-in ready.
