Why this belt

The factories around Oragadam, Sriperumbudur and Mahindra World City have pushed demand for land outward, and blocks of fifty or a hundred acres in one piece are now hard to find close to those clusters. Further south-west, around Uthiramerur, land is still held in larger holdings, and State Highway 118 between Pukkathurai on GST Road and Uthiramerur gives trucks a direct route out to the highway.

Large land here suits factories and industrial parks, warehousing and logistics, township or farm-plot layouts, educational campuses, and solar farms.

What to check on large land

  • Patta, chitta and A-register for every survey number, and that they all stand in the seller's name.
  • Land classification and the conversion needed for industrial or residential use.
  • Government land (poramboke), water channels or cart tracks crossing the block on the FMB sketch.
  • Buffer rules near lakes and tanks, and access permission from the state highway.
