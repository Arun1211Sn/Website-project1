Why buyers look at Thoraipakkam

Thoraipakkam sits on Old Mahabalipuram Road (Rajiv Gandhi Salai), the IT corridor of south Chennai, between Perungudi and Karapakkam. The Pallavaram to Thoraipakkam radial road links it across to GST Road and the airport side of the city, so the area works for people commuting both to the OMR offices and to the rest of Chennai.

That mix keeps demand steady for three kinds of property:

  • Rental buildings for people working in the OMR tech parks: flats, PG and serviced apartments.
  • Land parcels of two grounds and more, which developers need for apartment blocks with stilt parking.
  • Commercial frontage for shops, clinics and offices serving the neighbourhoods behind OMR.

What to check in Thoraipakkam

  • The CMDA master plan land-use zone and the width of the road in front, which together decide how much you can build.
  • That the plot is clear of road-widening lines and of the water bodies and marsh buffers that exist in parts of south Chennai.
  • For buildings, the sanctioned plan against the floors actually built, and who obtains the completion certificate.
  • Property tax assessment with the Greater Chennai Corporation, and a 30-year Encumbrance Certificate.
