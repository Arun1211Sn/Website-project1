Privacy Policy

How we handle information you share with Thirupathi S.T.S Groups Realty.

Last updated: 24 April 2026

1. Introduction

Thirupathi S.T.S Groups Realty ("we", "us", "our") operates the website thirupathirealestates.com (the "Service"). This page explains what information we collect from visitors, how we use it, and the choices available to you. By using our Service, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you submit our enquiry form or contact us via WhatsApp, phone, or email, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (if you choose to share it)
  • The service you are interested in
  • Any message or enquiry content you include

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we or our service providers may automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages viewed, time spent on pages, and referring URL
  • Device identifiers and cookie identifiers

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and calls
  • Provide property or service recommendations
  • Improve our website content and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Cookies and third-party services

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for analytics and advertising. The following third-party services may set cookies on your device:

  • Google AdSense — Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Google Analytics (if enabled) — used to understand how visitors interact with our site in aggregate.
You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Please note that some parts of the site may not function as intended without cookies.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the website (hosting, analytics)
  • Legal authorities, when required by law or to protect our rights
  • Property owners or buyers, only with your explicit consent, in connection with your enquiry

6. Data retention

We retain enquiry data for as long as is reasonably necessary to respond to you and, where applicable, complete a transaction. Analytics data is retained in aggregated form in accordance with the settings of the third-party services we use.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal exceptions
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

8. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no internet transmission or storage method is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
