About the property

On a half-ground plot, 2,500 sq ft of built-up area usually means two floors over the ground, which suits a family home with a rental unit, or two or three small rental flats. Anandham Nagar is a residential neighbourhood within Thoraipakkam, close to the OMR offices but away from main-road traffic.

Buying during construction lets you choose finishes, and sometimes the floor layout, before the work is complete. Ask us for the current stage of work and photos.

What to check before you buy

We help buyers collect and verify each of these before any advance is paid:

  • The approved building plan and that the built-up area and number of floors match it.
  • Title and Encumbrance Certificate for the plot.
  • A written schedule and cost for the remaining work, if the seller is completing it.
  • EB, water and sewer connection status.
