About the land

The plot sits directly on the GST Road service road at Kattankulathur, next to the Block Development Office. The owner's sketch shows a four-sided parcel: 170 ft along the service road, sides of 223 ft and 277 ft running back from the road, and roughly 159 ft at the rear. That works out to about 16 grounds.

Because the two sides are of different lengths, the plot is not a perfect rectangle. A buyer planning a building should have the boundaries measured against the government FMB sketch before fixing a layout. We arrange this as part of the site visit.

Who it suits

Continuous frontage of this length on GST Road is uncommon. The stretch between Tambaram and Chengalpattu has steady traffic all day, which makes the land a fit for uses that need visibility and truck access:

  • Showroom, dealership or retail outlet
  • Warehouse or distribution point serving the southern suburbs
  • School, hospital or clinic
  • Hotel, banquet hall or mixed commercial building

What to check before you buy

We help buyers collect and verify each of these before any advance is paid:

  • Fresh patta and chitta in the seller's name, matching the survey numbers in the parent documents.
  • Encumbrance Certificate for at least 30 years.
  • Which authority approves building plans here (CMDA or DTCP) and the land-use zone. Commercial use must be allowed for your plan.
  • Access from the service road: confirm the width and that no part of the frontage is earmarked for road widening.
  • An on-ground measurement against the FMB sketch, since the sides differ in length.
