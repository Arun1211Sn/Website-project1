About the land

Finding a hundred acres in one piece within an hour of Chennai is difficult: most land around the industrial belts is held in small parcels by many owners. This block is already contiguous, belongs to a single owner who is selling directly, sits on a state highway, and can grow by up to another 200 acres from adjoining holdings.

The land is reached from GST Road at the Pukkathurai junction: turn towards Uthiramerur and it is about six and a half kilometres along the highway. The site photos show level ground with young trees and internal mud tracks.

What to check before you buy

We help buyers collect and verify each of these before any advance is paid:

  • Every survey number's patta, chitta and A-register, all standing in the seller's name.
  • Land classification (nanjai or punjai) and the conversion needed for industrial or residential use.
  • Whether any government land (poramboke), water channel or cart track runs through the block on the FMB sketch.
  • Rules on development near the lake opposite, and the access permission from SH-118.
  • Encumbrance Certificates for each survey number.
