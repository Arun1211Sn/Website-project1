What to check before you buy

We help buyers collect and verify each of these before any advance is paid:

  • Every survey number's patta, chitta and A-register, and who the owners are. Large blocks are often held by several families.
  • Land classification (nanjai or punjai) and the conversion needed for industrial or residential use.
  • Whether any government land (poramboke), water channel or cart track runs through the block on the FMB sketch.
  • Rules on development near the lake opposite, and the access permission from SH-118.
  • Encumbrance Certificates for each survey number.
