About the land

Land parcels of nine thousand square feet are hard to find in the built-up stretch of OMR. Most of Thoraipakkam has already been divided into individual house plots of one or two grounds, so a single block of this size gives a developer room for a proper apartment building with parking, or a commercial building with frontage.

The area is close to the IT parks along OMR, so demand for rental housing, offices and daily-needs retail stays strong through the year.

What can be built

The building you can put up depends on the road width in front of the plot and the land-use zone in the CMDA master plan. As a rough guide for planning:

  • Residential apartments with stilt parking
  • An office building or IT/ITES floor space
  • Ground-floor shops with homes or offices above

We can share the road width and approach details on the site visit, and connect you with an architect for a feasibility check.

What to check before you buy

We help buyers collect and verify each of these before any advance is paid:

  • Patta, chitta and the parent documents for at least 30 years, with a current Encumbrance Certificate.
  • The land-use zone in the CMDA Second Master Plan and the width of the abutting road. Both decide the permissible floor area.
  • That the plot is not affected by a road-widening line or a water-body buffer.
  • Property tax assessment with the Greater Chennai Corporation.
