About the building

The frame, slabs and block walls are up; plastering, flooring, doors, electrical and plumbing are still to be done. That gives a buyer two advantages over a finished building: the price reflects the remaining work, and the interiors can be finished to suit the intended use.

Thoraipakkam lies on the OMR IT corridor, with tech parks, colleges and residential neighbourhoods close by. Buildings here typically go to rental housing for IT staff, serviced apartments, offices or a mix of shops below and homes above.

What to check before you buy

We help buyers collect and verify each of these before any advance is paid:

  • The sanctioned building plan and planning permit, and that the floors built match the floors approved.
  • Title documents and Encumbrance Certificate for the land the building stands on.
  • A structural engineer's check of the frame before you take over the remaining work.
  • Whether a completion certificate will be needed for occupation and electricity/water connections, and who obtains it.
  • Status of the EB service connection, water supply and sewer connection.
