About the land

Sriperumbudur sits on the Chennai–Bangalore Highway, the busiest manufacturing corridor in Tamil Nadu, with car, electronics and component plants on both sides of the road. Large, regular plots with road frontage are hard to find here, and most land that comes up still needs conversion and filling before anything can be built.

This block has already been converted and filled, and building approval is ready for about 2.15 lakh sq ft. It has long frontage on two roads, a 50 ft road and a 40 ft road, so trucks can come in on one side and a separate gate can serve offices or staff. The new Chennai–Bengaluru expressway runs next to it.

The land is held by a company. A buyer can take it over by buying the company rather than through a sale deed, which changes how stamp duty and registration apply. Take your own legal and tax advice on that route before you decide.

What to check before you buy

We help buyers collect and verify each of these before any advance is paid:

  • The company's ownership of every survey number, with patta and chitta in its name.
  • If you buy the company: its accounts, liabilities, loans and any pending cases, through a full due diligence.
  • The conversion order and the building approval, and how long the approval stays valid.
  • Access rights from both roads, and any setback or widening line for the 40 ft and 50 ft roads.
  • High-tension lines, channels or water bodies near the boundary, on the survey plan and on the ground.
