About the land

Thervoy Kandigai, in Gummidipoondi taluk north of Chennai, is home to a SIPCOT industrial park, and the Gummidipoondi and Kavaraipettai area has grown into an industrial belt with easy access to the Chennai–Kolkata highway and the northern ports.

This land is about 15.5 acres in the red zone, which is meant for industrial use. It is made up of several adjoining parcels, shown in colour on the sketch, and an approach road runs from the parcels out to the main road. The owner is open to discussing the price.

Suited to

  • Factories and heavy industry that need a red zone location
  • Warehousing for the Gummidipoondi and SIPCOT industrial units
  • Ancillary units and suppliers near the industrial park

  • Ownership of the approach road, or a registered right of way over it, all the way to the main road.
  • Encumbrance Certificates for every parcel.
