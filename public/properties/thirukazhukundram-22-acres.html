What to check before you buy

We help buyers collect and verify each of these before any advance is paid:

  • Patta, chitta and A-register for every survey number, in the seller's name.
  • Land classification (nanjai or punjai), and the conversion needed if you plan a layout or non-farm use.
  • Any agricultural land ceiling or eligibility rules that apply to you as a buyer.
  • Water source on the land (borewell, open well or channel) and the free-supply electricity connection, if any, and whether it transfers.
  • Road access from the Thirukazhukundram to Chengalpattu road, and that no government land or water channel crosses the block on the FMB sketch.
  • Encumbrance Certificates for each survey number.
