Land units used in Tamil Nadu

Chennai and the surrounding districts mix several units. City plots are sold in square feet or grounds; suburban and village land in cents and acres; and government records such as patta and the FMB sketch use hectares and ares.

1 square metre10.76 sq ft
1 square yard9 sq ft
1 ground2,400 sq ft
1 cent435.60 sq ft
1 acre43,560 sq ft
1 are1,076.39 sq ft
1 hectare107,639.10 sq ft
  • Ground: 2,400 sq ft, the usual unit for plots inside Chennai. A 60 × 40 ft plot is exactly one ground.
  • Cent: one hundredth of an acre, 435.6 sq ft. Common for plots outside the city and for farm land.
  • Acre: 100 cents, or about 18 grounds.
  • Hectare and are: the metric units on patta and chitta extracts. One hectare is 100 ares, or about 2.47 acres.

Some older documents use local units such as the kuzhi or kani, whose size differs from district to district. Convert those only with the figure written in the document itself, or ask us to check.

Comparing prices? A rate per cent and a rate per sq ft can look very different for the same land. Convert both to one unit before you compare. See our current listings or read the land buying checklist.
